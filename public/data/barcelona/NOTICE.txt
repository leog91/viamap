Source: Ajuntament de Barcelona / Open Data BCN, Transports públics.
https://opendata-ajuntament.barcelona.cat/data/dataset/transports
Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
ViaMap filters to metro, FGC, rail and tram entries, removes identical entries
and formats the location labels. No published schedule or live status is used.

Network overlay: Ferrocarrils de la Generalitat de Catalunya (FGC),
Rutes GTFS_routes (CC BY 4.0).
https://dadesobertes.fgc.cat/explore/assets/gtfs_routes/
ViaMap selects Barcelona-area lines and splits published multipart geometry
for display. The overlay does not cover the other Barcelona operators.

Timetable source: Renfe Data, Horarios cercanías (CC BY 4.0).
https://data.renfe.com/dataset/horarios-cercanias
ViaMap selected trips boardable within its Barcelona study area, reformatted
stop and timetable information, and simplified the matched route shapes.
Cancellations or replacement services may differ from published schedules.
