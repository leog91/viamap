ViaMap reformats the published entrance coordinates, station, line, address
and direction fields. Network line geometry is from SBASE / BA Data:
https://data.buenosaires.gob.ar/dataset/subte-estaciones (same licence).
ViaMap reformats the GeoJSON and assigns display colours. No published
schedule or live status is used.
